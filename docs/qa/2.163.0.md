# Source tag 2.163.0: manga text line by line, checked on a real volume, 2026-11-19

Base: `v2.162.1`. The maintainer tried manga read ahead on a real volume (Nana volume 1, a Japanese digital release of 760×1200 pages) and reported three problems:

- the text written over the balloons was too small in some of them;
- the white boxes covered the art;
- reading had become slower.

The maintainer then asked for 30 pages to be checked, comparing the text's size, its place, and how completely the original is covered (D-82).

## How it was checked

- **Run:** `l7_a_real_manga_is_read_ahead` (new) opens the volume in the real app, under a virtual KWin with the real models. It walks pages 10–39 and, for every block, prints the text, the lines and the covers, with a screenshot of each page.
- **Measuring script:** a script outside the repository read those lines against the original pictures. For each block it reported:
  - how much ink near the text was left uncovered;
  - the size of the characters written, against the width of the original line;
  - how much of each line they spanned.
- **Review by eye:** the script also drew the overlay beside the original at full size, and the worst blocks were looked at side by side.

## What was found, and changed

1. **Lines.** The block detector groups balloons that touch into one block, and the reader then lost whole balloons. The detector's line map now gives the lines, and each line is read on its own.
   - All of a block's lines go through the model together: one encoder batch, then each decoder step for all of them at once.
   - On a six-column block this took 2.3 s, against 4.3 s for the lines one by one and 1.85 s for the block whole, which had lost a balloon.
2. **Short columns marked as one, and specks.** The detector sometimes marks two or three short columns as a single blob, and sometimes marks a 1-pixel speck. Specks are dropped and blobs are split by their ink.
3. **Faint strokes.** Thin grey strokes fell above the fixed ink grey of 128. Ink is now judged against each block's own ground.
4. **Bands and outlines.** Each mark's band is limited:
   - to lines alongside it, which leaves out other balloons above or below;
   - to the block, with a little room for furigana.

   A balloon's outline crossing the band no longer joins columns. A column the detector left unmarked is added only between two marked ones, as wide and as dense in ink as they are.
5. **Covers.** One box over a whole block covered the art between its balloons. Each line now has its own box, and all the boxes of a page are drawn before any text.
6. **Size.** The size of a balloon's text was the one its narrowest line held, so one bad line shrank the whole balloon. It is now the size most of its lines hold.
7. **Speed.** Detection with the line map takes about 0.6 s a page, and laying out about 50 ms. Reading takes about 1–2 s per balloon; it was the cost already, and is about 1.3–1.5 times what it was. The default number of pages read ahead goes from 10 to 3.
8. **Cache.** The page cache is versioned by layout, so pages read before are read again once.

## Measurements, 30 pages (356 blocks)

| | First run | 2.163.0 |
|---|---|---|
| Size of the characters / original line width: median, 10th percentile | 0.93, 0.49 (some under 0.1) | 0.93, 0.77 |
| Blocks written in columns | 354 | 352 |

- **Uncovered ink:** the measure of ink left near a block cannot tell text from art, so it was judged by eye on the worst blocks. In 2.163.0 their original text is covered, and what the measure counts is the art beside the balloons.
- **What remains comes from the detector:**
  - It sometimes groups two touching balloons. Their lines are then read correctly, but the text is written in one order across both.
  - One column was covered with no text, because the reader returned nothing for it.

## Not changed

- The OCR model has no export with a key/value cache in the pinned repository, so the decoder still runs over the whole text at each step.
- The manual box read is unchanged.
