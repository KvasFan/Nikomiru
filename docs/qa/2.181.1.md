# 2.181.1: an ellipsis written three dots to a cell, no white where nothing was read, 2026-11-21

Base: `v2.181.0` (tagged, not published). The maintainer reported, with a screenshot of Kaiji volume 1 p.25, that the kanji of 錯覚する…… were not shown readably over the balloon.

The text had been read right (`そこのところを` / `錯覚する．．．．．．`), but manga-ocr writes "…" as three dots, and each dot took a cell: the column counted ten cells for the six of the picture, and its characters were drawn small enough to fit. Runs of two or more dots now share cells three at a time, drawn as middle dots along the cell (T-MNG-012, `an_ellipsis_takes_a_cell_per_three_dots`).

Checked in the real app on that page (`l7_a_real_manga_is_read_ahead`, page 25, release build): 錯覚する at the size of the picture's characters and the dots in two cells, as in the original.

Then the maintainer asked that boxes where the reader finds nothing keep their text. A block read as nothing at all already got no region, but a line of a block read as nothing kept its white cover: the picture's text there was hidden with nothing written over it. Its cover is now left out (a cover lying mostly over a line read as nothing and over none read), while the covers of the lines read and of their furigana stay (`a_line_read_as_nothing_keeps_the_picture`); the page cache's version goes to 16, so pages are read again once.
