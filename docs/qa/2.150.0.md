# Source tag 2.150.0 — M127, 2026-11-18

Base: source tag `v2.140.0` / `8f56fc4`. The maintainer asked for the main page in tabs, no overlapping text and a better look, for manga words looked up directly, and for furigana of web pages to stay on their line (D-77).

- **Shelves (VID-001, VID-002, BOK-004).** The main page has these tabs: Collection, Videos, Books, Manga, Websites, then Statistics, the Review and media servers. The Videos tab holds the history of videos opened on their own and saved links. Books, manga and web pages each have their own tab, and closing the reader goes back to it. The tools are in one menu.
- **No overlapping text.** The tabs and each tab's actions have rows of their own. In list rows, episode rows and the reading page's header, the buttons are laid out first and the text is cut before them. The series controls fit on one wrapping row.
- **Manga (MNG-010).** A click on a word of a Mokuro page looks it up through Mokuro's line boxes. A box dragged over a page is read and its first word opens in the dictionary. Without the model, the drag says the model is needed instead of doing nothing.
- **Furigana (WEB-004).** HTML white space is collapsed, and `</rt>` may be left out. Readings are placed from the glyphs themselves, so a word that ends its line keeps its reading on that line.

## Note on the CBZ report

In the actual app with the stand-in text and with the real model, a drag over a CBZ page reads the text. The text was then shown above the page, but nothing opened by itself, and without the model a drag did nothing. Both have changed: the dictionary now opens on the first word, and a drag without the model shows a message.
