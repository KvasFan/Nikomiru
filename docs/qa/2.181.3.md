# 2.181.3: text over the art read, 2026-11-22

Base: `v2.181.2` (tagged, not published). The maintainer reported, with a screenshot of Kaiji volume 1 p.49, that この男の言うこと当たってる……, written over Kaiji's face rather than in a balloon, was not read.

The page cache had no region there: the detector found the block (centre 102,194, about 100 × 155) only at 0.34, under the 0.4 it is held to, and its line map marked no column in it. Blocks found at 0.25 or more are now kept as doubtful when they touch no surer block, and their text is written over the page only when it has at least two letters and every letter, dots and marks aside, is read at least 90 % sure.

The four sets of 2.180.0 plus that page, every doubtful block read and judged against the picture:

| Doubtful blocks | Kept | Left out |
|---|---|---|
| 33 | この男の言うこと当たってる…… (letters 0.99), 第７４話 (a chapter title, Kaiji 7 p.57), 営業スマイツ (Nana 1 p.27, really 営業スマイル; ツ at 0.91), ２・１ (Kaiji 1 p.24, a page number) | 29: runs of dots, drawn sounds (ああぁぁ…, ピロピロ), small scrawls and art read at 0.05–0.89 |

Judging by letters only, the short columns of 2.181.2 were checked again on the same 22 strips: the same eight columns kept (でも, から, 以上, 時, 場所, 今, 先生, あと) and いた, no outline. Page cache version 18.

Checked in the real app on Kaiji 1 p.49 (`l7_a_real_manga_is_read_ahead`, release build): the block reads この男の言うこと．．．当たってる．．． and is written over the picture.

## Final regression

`cargo xtask ci --full`: **all green**, with **146 actual GUI cases passed**.
