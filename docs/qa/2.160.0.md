# Source tag 2.160.0 — manga pages read ahead, and zoom, 2026-11-18

Base: release `v2.151.0`. The maintainer reported that OCR was still slow, and asked for the page shown and the next one to be read ahead (D-78, M128, MNG-011).

## What changed

- **Detector:** dmMaze's comic-text-detector, as ONNX: GPL-3.0, 90 MiB, pinned on Hugging Face at `a5d67ec`. It sits in the model folder beside manga-ocr. Reading a page means finding its balloons first, and manga-ocr reads only a box already cut out.
- **Speed:** under tract, the detector's block output alone runs in about 0.27 s on a 1200×1700 page. With the mask heads it took 0.86 s, so they are left out of the plan. Each block then takes about 1 s on a loaded machine, and the two balloons of the test page are read in about 2.7 s in all. This is measured with `examples/detect_bench.rs` in an optimised build, at a load of about 10.
- **Order:** the page shown is read first, then the next turn's pages, one at a time on a thread of their own. A box the reader drags goes first: the page thread waits between blocks.
- **Cache:** pages read are kept in the cache folder under `manga-pages`, keyed by the picture's SHA-256 and the two models' revisions.
- **Fetching:** the reading page offers the detector alone to anyone who already has manga-ocr. The setup and the first fetch take both models.

## Zoom (RDR-005)

- **Books:** Ctrl and the wheel, or a pinch, scale every measure of the reader: modes, pages and vertical columns. The page draws at the new size at once, and the setting `[books] text_zoom` follows in steps of 5 %, from 60 % to 250 %. The pagination key includes the size.
- **Manga:**
  - Ctrl and the wheel enlarge the pages shown about the pointer, up to 4×, kept on the view.
  - Enlarged, the wheel and a middle-button drag move the page instead of turning it.
  - A turned page opens at its top right.
  - Balloons are clipped to the view and still clicked.
  - The enlargement is not kept.
- **Fix:** Ctrl and the wheel no longer turn a page.
- **Tests (L3):** zooming a vertical book; zooming, moving, clicking and unzooming a manga page.

## Checks

- **L1:** the blocks kept, the guessed columns, and the cache round trip.
- **L3:** the reading line and the read-ahead regions are clicked; the detector offer works.

- **L7:** the real detector and reader find both vertical balloons of a page drawn in Noto CJK and read 「明日も食べました」. This takes 22 s in the unoptimised test build.

## 2.161.0 — the text written on the page (MNG-012)

- **Request:** the maintainer asked for the text found to be written in its place, at about the size of the picture's.
- **Layout:**
  - Each balloon is covered by an opaque light ground, and its lines go in their boxes, columns from the right.
  - One size for the balloon: as wide as its narrowest column and no longer than every line holds. Characters are spread along the line, at most a fifth apart.
- **Clicks:** a click looks up the character written under the pointer, and the word looked up is marked on it.
- **Switch:** **Text on the page** shows the drawing alone, kept as `[books] manga_text`.
- **First try, by eye:** the L7 screenshots showed characters smaller than the drawing's, packed at the top of each column, with the drawing faintly showing through a 96 % ground.
- **Fix:** the characters now take the column's whole width and are spread along it, on an opaque ground. The second screenshots match the drawing's size and place.

## 2.162.0 — read further ahead, a dictionary that stays put (MNG-013, MNG-014)

- **Reading ahead:** after the pages shown, *Pages read ahead* pages (default 10) are read in order. Their text is kept within *Memory for the text read* (default 32 MB).
  - A page's text takes a few kilobytes, so the memory is rarely what limits.
  - Past the limit, the pages farthest from those shown go first, and no more are read until a page is turned.
  - The pages forgotten stay in the disk cache.
- **The dictionary in manga:** it has a **⠿ Move** handle. Its place is kept as shares of the window in `[books] manga_popup`, and ↺ puts it back next to the word.
- **Checks:**
  - L1: forgetting pages, and the settings' bounds and reset.
  - L3: moving, keeping and resetting the dictionary.
  - L6: with 2 pages ahead, three pages are read, then one more on a turn.
  - L7 screenshot: the dictionary kept at the top right, with the word marked on the balloon.
