# 2.181.0: Android without its gaps, manga lines read with their balloon, 2026-11-21

Base: `v2.180.0`. The maintainer asked to make sure of AnkiDroid, FFmpeg's command line and the keyring on Android, the player's bar on an upright phone and the manga reader's misread characters (D-85).

## Android

On the Android 15 emulator of 2.180.0 (x86_64, SwiftShader GLES 3), with the APK of `cargo xtask android --ffmpeg` (`--debug` for `run-as`) and AnkiDroid 2.25.3 (its x86_64 APK from its GitHub release):

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| FFmpeg | At the start the app links `files/bin/ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` to the APK's `libffmpeg.so`/`libffprobe.so` and finds FFmpeg 8.1.3 through `NIKOMIRU_FFMPEG`; only the AV1 and VP9 encoders are missing (animated AVIF and WebM fall back). Run from the app's own user, `ffmpeg -version` works. |
| Embedded subtitles | With the sidecar `.srt` removed, `av_basic.mkv`'s embedded Japanese track is extracted and listed in the subtitle list; a word in it looks up. |
| A scaled frame | The first build's x86_64 FFmpeg (no nasm, inline assembly left on) scaled pictures wrongly: thumbnails and card pictures came out striped. Built without any x86 assembly, a 640-pixel frame is byte for byte the one Linux's FFmpeg makes. |
| AnkiDroid | The Anki page with `ankidroid://` (the 2.180.0 address saved on the phone migrated): Android's request for AnkiDroid's permission, allowed; **Create Lapis deck** made the note type and the deck in AnkiDroid; two cards mined from the subtitle list, each with its `.m4a` sentence audio and `.jpg` picture in AnkiDroid's `collection.media`, the app's copies gone from its cache; the card shown in AnkiDroid's review with its word, reading, meaning, sentence, picture, audio and tags. |
| Keyring | `keyring = true` with a Jimaku key in the file: at the start the key moved to `keyring.json` (nonce and AES-GCM text, no plain key) and left `config.toml`; after a restart the settings show the key's 18 characters. |
| Upright phone | The bar: Library, play, the time, 字, List and ⋯ in one row; the menu holds the rest. The settings fill the screen with the tab list at the top, opaque; the library's first steps wrap. |
| No folder of this computer | `strings` of the APK's FFmpeg libraries finds no `/home`; `ffmpeg -version` shows `--prefix=/nikomiru` and bare tool names. |

The narrow bar has its own GUI test at 640 points (T-PLY-024), which opens the settings from the menu.

## Manga

The four sets of 2.180.0 (Kaiji 1 pp. 5–24, Kaiji 7 pp. 40–69, Nana 1 pp. 10–39, Nana 11 pp. 60–89: 1,062 blocks, 712 with more than one line), read outside the app with the same detector, layout and line crops (a throw-away example program, not kept), every changed line judged by eye against the picture.

| Tried | What changed | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| int8 → fp32 model | Greedy readings 12.1 % of characters from manga-ocr's own output (fp32 with its beam search), int8 11.7 % | No gain worth 4 × the download |
| manga-ocr's beam search (4 beams, no three tokens repeated, length penalty 2) | 0.6 % from the reference, but its no-repeat rule cuts ハハハハ to バハハハ and runs of dots to three | Dropped |
| The same beam search without the no-repeat rule | On 104 blocks of Kaiji only the count of dots changed; four times the decoder's time | Dropped |
| Line crops widened (with the page, or with white) | カムー → カなー; the page's neighbouring columns leak in | Dropped |
| The balloon read whole, its characters taken where the readings align | 104 lines changed: about 30 better, 39 worse (the whole reading's own dakuten slips: プ/ブ, デ/テ, ジ/シ) | Dropped |
| The same, keeping the character the model was surer of | 38 lines changed on the pages concerned: about 25 better (かなー, 一目惚れ, 偽名, さわやか, シブイ, 容体, 興奮, デビュー, 大喜び, カジュアル, 誰か, なんの…), 8 worse (日惚れ, テビュー, ゴンピュータ, けしからい…), the rest punctuation | **Kept** |
| … only for balloons with a character under 80 % sure | 36 of the 38 kept (one of the worse ones goes); the balloon read whole for 45 % of the multi-line balloons on those pages | **Kept**, `UNSURE = 0.8` |

Time: reading lines took 1,385 s for the 1,062 blocks, reading every multi-line balloon whole 1,262 s more; with the 80 % rule about a third more than before. The drawn sound effect of Nana 11 p.60 is still read as words, and some names stay wrong in both readings (蝋燭, 章司).
