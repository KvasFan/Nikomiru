# 2.182.0: the look of Fushi 3, 2026-11-23

Base: `v2.181.3`. The maintainer asked to improve the interface after the latest Fushi (D-86). The latest stable Fushi is 3.3.0 (tag `v3.3.0`, commit `87f1c45`); it was looked at in two ways:

- **Running.** The installed Fushi 3.3.0 in an off-screen KWin (1600 × 900) with the maintainer's Fushi library: the home page, the Video page (Home, Series, All videos), the settings (Appearance and interaction). Its video player could not be shown: the library's episode is streamed from a media server the off-screen session cannot reach.
- **Source.** The README screenshots of the tag (older, still named Hibiki) and its Material 3 Expressive notes (`docs/agent/material-ui-upgrade.md`).

What Nikomiru takes from it (SET-010): a navigation rail with icons and a pill for the item shown; surfaces tinted by the accent stepping up from the page; pill-shaped buttons, fields and tabs; a light primary action under dark text in the dark theme; a tonal "chosen"; larger targets; banners on a tonal fill; covers with round corners; a large card of what is being watched with its title over the picture and a wavy progress line; the settings' tabs with icons and their sections' names in the accent. Left out: Fushi's decorative background shapes, its springs and page transitions, the shape-morphing of its rail icons.
