## Scope and remaining checks

Chapter analysis accepts at most 1,024 nonempty text paragraphs, 256 KiB of
text and 64 KiB per paragraph. Other chapters retain their normal reader
and eligible visible word colours, without a partial score or word list.
Manga image chapters do not run this analysis. Completed counts survive
segmentation-cache eviction and use the shared current word statuses.

The score applies to the chapter being read. Whole-book scores before
opening, scores on series cards, embedded-track episode scores and new words
met per episode remain on M21's roadmap. A real Japanese book with native
ruby and a full dictionary still needs a manual score comparison. The
Windows cross-target check verifies compilation, not native runtime UI.

No Rust dependency, database schema or persisted configuration field was added.
