# 2.181.2: a short last column read, 2026-11-22

Base: `v2.181.1`. The maintainer reported, with a screenshot of Kaiji volume 1 p.41, that いた, the last column of もっと利口なやり方があれば……と誰もが考えていた, was not read.

The detector's line map marked five of the six columns; the existing search for unmarked columns measured the block's ink without the marks, and against columns six characters long the two characters of いた reached the 15 % threshold only in a 3-pixel strip (its vertical strokes), which was taken for furigana and dropped. Such a strip is now measured again over its own length (`widen`): いた comes out 19 pixels wide, as wide as the other columns, and is added.

On the four sets of 2.180.0 (Kaiji 1 pp. 5–24, Kaiji 7 pp. 40–69, Nana 1 pp. 10–39, Nana 11 pp. 60–89; 1,064 blocks), the layout with and without the change, every changed block looked at with the new line drawn:

| Version | Lines added | Text | Not text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Widened strips kept as dense as the lines | 21 in 21 blocks (and one doubled, then removed) | でも, から, 以上, 時, 場所, その, 今, 先生, いーな, あと, まーね | 10 pieces of balloon outlines in Nana, one row partly doubled |
| Ink going on past the strip's ends taken for an outline | 13 | 7 | 4, and その, 先生, いーな lost: dropped |
| **The reading at least 90 % sure in every character** | read by the model: the text ones at 0.99–1.00 (いた 1.00), the outlines at 0.13–0.74 (！, ン, へ, ．．．) | でも, から, 以上, 時, 場所, 今, 先生, あと | none; いーな (0.68), まーね (0.36) and その (read この, 0.66) left out |

Checked in the real app on Kaiji 1 p.41 (`l7_a_real_manga_is_read_ahead`, release build): the block reads もっと利口なやり方があれば．．．．と誰もが考えていた and いた is written over its column. Page cache version 17.
